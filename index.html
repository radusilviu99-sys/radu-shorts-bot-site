✦ Radu Shorts BotPersonal desktop project
Educational short-form video workflow

Curiosity, made short.

Radu Shorts Bot is a personal tool that researches topics, prepares English-language educational quizzes and fact videos, and can upload the resulting Shorts to the Google account owner's YouTube channel after authorization.

Discover

Finds potential educational topics in public sources, including geography, science, nature and history. Source references are kept for review.

Create

Builds vertical, text-led Shorts using reusable visual materials, licensed music, animations and interactive questions—without voice-over.

Upload

Uses the YouTube Data API's youtube.upload permission to upload videos to the account explicitly authorized by the owner.

Google account access: The app requests YouTube upload access only when the owner chooses to authorize it. It is run locally by the owner and is not an open service for other users. See the Privacy Policy for details and revocation instructions.
