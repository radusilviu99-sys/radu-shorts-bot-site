Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Radu Shorts Bot is a personal, locally operated desktop application. This page explains how the application accesses and uses Google user data when its owner authorizes YouTube uploading.

1. Information accessed

The application requests the Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. This permission allows it to upload video files and associated metadata to the YouTube channel authorized by the account owner. The application receives OAuth access credentials and information returned by the YouTube upload operation, such as the uploaded video ID.

2. How the information is used

Authorization is used solely to upload videos selected or generated by the owner to the authorized YouTube channel, and to authenticate those upload requests. The application does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

3. Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer in a token.json file so the app can continue authorized uploads without requiring login every time. Generated media, upload metadata and logs may also be stored locally. Access to these files depends on the security of the owner's Windows account and computer.

4. Sharing and disclosure

OAuth credentials and Google user data are not sold or shared with advertisers. Authorized upload requests are transmitted to Google's YouTube Data API. Video files and their metadata are stored by YouTube and may become visible to others depending on the publication setting chosen by the owner. The application separately accesses public research and media sources for content creation; it does not send OAuth credentials to those sources.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally stored authorization credentials remain until the owner deletes them or revokes access. The owner can revoke the application's access through Google Account permissions and delete token.json from the application folder. Previously uploaded videos can be managed or deleted through YouTube Studio.

6. Scope and changes

This is a personal-use application, not a public sign-up service. If its permissions or data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe the new behavior before the new use takes place.

7. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the application owner using the support email shown on the Google OAuth authorization screen.
